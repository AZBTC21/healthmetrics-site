Frequently Asked Questions

Why isn't VitalMtrx showing any data?
VitalMtrx reads data from the Apple Health app. Make sure you have granted VitalMtrx permission to read Health data by going to Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → VitalMtrx and enabling the categories you want tracked. Data will appear after the app has been given access and Apple Health has recorded entries for those categories.
How is my Readiness Score calculated?
Your Readiness Score (0–100) is a weighted average of five categories: Sleep (30%), Recovery (25%), Activity (20%), Body & Fitness (15%), and Consistency (10%). You can adjust these weights in the Settings tab and see a live preview of how the change affects your score.
How does the training recommendation work?
Each morning, VitalMtrx gives you one of four directives: Push Hard, Train Normally, Easy Day, or Rest & Recover. Unlike the overall Readiness Score, the recommendation is based solely on your sleep quality and HRV — the signals captured before the day begins. This way, the recommendation is accurate even if you check it first thing in the morning before any activity has been recorded. The same recommendation appears on Apple Watch for a quick glance without opening the app.
Does VitalMtrx upload my health data anywhere?
No. VitalMtrx is entirely on-device. All data is read from Apple Health and processed locally. Nothing is sent to any server, and no account is required to use the app.
How far back does the app show data?
VitalMtrx fetches up to 60 days of health history. The Trends tab lets you view charts across 7, 30, 60, 90, or 180-day windows — longer periods show historical workout data and trend patterns.
Why is my HRV or sleep data missing?
HRV and detailed sleep data require a compatible Apple Watch. If you wear your Apple Watch while sleeping and during rest, it will record the data that VitalMtrx needs. Make sure automatic sleep detection or sleep tracking is enabled in the Watch app or Health app on your iPhone.
How does the Apple Watch companion work?
The VitalMtrx Watch app shows your Readiness Score, training recommendation, and all five component bars (Sleep, Recovery, Activity, Body, Consistency) directly on your wrist. Data is sent from your iPhone to the Watch automatically when both devices are nearby. The Watch complication lets you see your score at a glance from any watch face.
How do I delete my data?
VitalMtrx reads from Apple Health and keeps a local cache on your device. To delete Health records themselves, open the Health app → Browse → [category] → Show All Data → Edit → Delete. Revoke VitalMtrx in the Health app to stop new HealthKit reads. Deleting VitalMtrx removes the app and its local cache from that device; underlying health records remain in Apple Health until you delete them there. Health app → Browse → [category] → Show All Data → Edit → Delete. Deleting the VitalMtrx app removes the app itself and any locally cached preferences; all underlying health records remain in Apple Health until you delete them there.
The app crashed or something isn't working right. What should I do?
First, try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, try revoking and re-granting Health permissions in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices. Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026 — same policy as in the VitalMtrx app. Full page: privacy.html.

Apple Health data we read. VitalMtrx requests read-only access to Apple Health (HealthKit). Depending on what you have recorded and what you allow, that can include sleep, heart and recovery metrics, activity (steps, calories, distance, stand hours), workouts and GPS routes, body and fitness measures such as weight and VO2 max, and rhythm-related data (including AFib burden or irregular-rhythm events) only when those metrics appear in the app. We use this data to compute your readiness score, trends, insights, and workout details on your device.

What we do not do. VitalMtrx does not write to Apple Health. There are no accounts, no VitalMtrx servers, and no sale of your data. Health information is not uploaded to us. You do not create a VitalMtrx login.

Where data lives. Health snapshots stay on this device as local cache files in the app’s Documents folder. Settings (units, goals, score weights, and similar preferences) are stored in UserDefaults on the device. If you use the Apple Watch app or Home Screen / Lock Screen widgets, a small readiness snapshot is shared through an App Group on your devices so those surfaces can update without sending data to VitalMtrx.

Optional features. If you enable notifications, scheduling stays on-device (for example morning readiness or workout recap reminders). If you save a share card, VitalMtrx may add an image to Photos using add-only access — it does not read your library. If you turn on AI Insights, summaries run on-device with Apple Intelligence and are not sent to VitalMtrx.

How to stop. Revoke VitalMtrx in the Health app to stop new HealthKit reads. Deleting VitalMtrx removes the app and its local cache from that device. Watch and widget data in the App Group is removed when you delete the app (or the Watch app) from the device.

Changes. We may update this policy as the app changes. This version was last updated on August 25, 2026. Continued use after an in-app update means you accept the revised policy. Review it in Settings whenever you like.
