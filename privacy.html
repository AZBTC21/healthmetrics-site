Privacy Policy

Last updated August 25, 2026. This is the same policy shown in the VitalMtrx app.

Apple Health data we read

VitalMtrx requests read-only access to Apple Health (HealthKit). Depending on what you have recorded and what you allow, that can include sleep, heart and recovery metrics, activity (steps, calories, distance, stand hours), workouts and GPS routes, body and fitness measures such as weight and VO2 max, and rhythm-related data (including AFib burden or irregular-rhythm events) only when those metrics appear in the app. We use this data to compute your readiness score, trends, insights, and workout details on your device.

What we do not do

VitalMtrx does not write to Apple Health. There are no accounts, no VitalMtrx servers, and no sale of your data. Health information is not uploaded to us. You do not create a VitalMtrx login.

Where data lives

Health snapshots stay on this device as local cache files in the app’s Documents folder. Settings (units, goals, score weights, and similar preferences) are stored in UserDefaults on the device. If you use the Apple Watch app or Home Screen / Lock Screen widgets, a small readiness snapshot is shared through an App Group on your devices so those surfaces can update without sending data to VitalMtrx.

Optional features

If you enable notifications, scheduling stays on-device (for example morning readiness or workout recap reminders). If you save a share card, VitalMtrx may add an image to Photos using add-only access — it does not read your library. If you turn on AI Insights, summaries run on-device with Apple Intelligence and are not sent to VitalMtrx.

How to stop

Revoke VitalMtrx in the Health app to stop new HealthKit reads. Deleting VitalMtrx removes the app and its local cache from that device. Watch and widget data in the App Group is removed when you delete the app (or the Watch app) from the device.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. This version was last updated on August 25, 2026. Continued use after an in-app update means you accept the revised policy. Review it in Settings whenever you like.
